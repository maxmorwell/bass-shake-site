Bass Shake makes your video move with the bass, as if the camera were sitting on the speaker.

  1. Load a clip with bass in it.
  2. Set the shake to taste: strength, wobble, blur.
  3. Save it, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Free, and it runs in your browser. Your clips never leave your phone or computer.
