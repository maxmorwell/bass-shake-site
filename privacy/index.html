Privacy

Your videos

They never leave your device. Bass Shake processes them in your browser, and we never see your video, its sound or its name.

Counting visits

We count visits and app use anonymously, to see what works: pages visited, where visitors came from, country and device type, which steps and buttons are used, errors, the settings chosen, and rough clip details such as length band and orientation. No cookies, and nothing that identifies you. The counting service is Umami, hosted in the EU.

Remembered in this browser.

Feedback

The feedback box sends only when you tap Send, and only what it lists: your answers and a technical report covering settings, device, browser and timings. Never your video or its sound.

Mailing list

If you join, we keep your email address, the date you joined, and your timezone and language, so we can send news and beta invites. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share the list. The list is stored with Google.
